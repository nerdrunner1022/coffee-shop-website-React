# Vibe Coding Raw Mind Dump

## Core Vision & Concept
- This is a website for a coffee shop named Beans in a Pot.
- This website establishes an online presence for the physical storefront of the shop, allowing customers to get to know about the business, their menu and offerings, their availability and the extra services they provide and  accomodate within their spaces.

## User Flow & Vibe
- A user should feel warm, right at home with the feeling of a warm cup of coffee in their hands when they use the website.
- The user lands on their homepage with an image of a warm cup of coffee piping hot with a gentle column of steam blowing from the coffee cup, and a warm message welcoming the user. The user scrolls down to view the services that the shop provides — selling a variety of coffee beans and blends for those who like, breakfast and tea time meals, hosting birthday parties for children, adults and older people, as well as, mental-cool-down rooms and more relaxing services. Under that section, the user is greeted with offers of the day, which is then followed by a peek of their menu. The user is shown the full menu on another separate page afterwards, and can be returned back to the homepage. Underneath the menu peek, a little newsletter box, and then the footer.

## Technical Notes & Constraints
 - **Frontend Technologies**: React, Tailwind CSS (Vercel frontend hosting)
 - **Backend Technologies**: NestJS (Vercel backend hosting)
 - **Database**: PostgreSQL, NeonDB as provider
 - **ORM**: Prisma
 - **Authentication**: JWT
 - **API Style**: REST API
 - This app is going to be purely on the front-end. I want a premium look on the website, with a storytelling feel to the website — sections of the website render into view as the user scrolls to the viewport, similar to Apple's official website. A sample look on the homepage hero section is shown by the mockup in this location: `~/Projects/Internship/coffee-shop/docs/inbox/coffee-mockup.jpg`.
