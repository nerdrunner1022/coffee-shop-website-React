## Espresso-Based (Short & Strong)

### Espresso

A concentrated 30 ml (1 oz) shot of coffee extracted under high pressure, serving as the foundation for most café drinks.

### Ristretto

A shorter, more concentrated espresso shot pulled with half the water, yielding a sweeter, less bitter flavor.

### Lungo

A “stretched” espresso where twice as much water is passed through the grounds, resulting in a lighter body with more bitter notes.

### Doppio

Simply a double shot of espresso (60 ml / 2 oz), standard in most modern specialty coffee shops.

### Espresso Macchiato

A shot of espresso “stained” (marked) with just a small dollop of warm milk foam on top.

## Milk & Espresso Combinations

### Cortado

Equal parts espresso and warm steamed milk (1:1 ratio) with minimal foam, reducing acidity while keeping a strong coffee punch.

### Cappuccino

Equal thirds of espresso, steamed milk, and a thick layer of airy foam (traditionally 1:1:1 ratio).

### Flat White

Originating in Australia/New Zealand, this pairs a double shot of espresso (or ristretto) with silky microfoam, producing a velvet texture without heavy foam.

### Caffè Latte

A mild, creamy drink made of espresso, a generous amount of steamed milk, and a thin layer of foam on top (typically 1:3 to 1:5 ratio).

### Latte Macchiato

“Spotted milk” — steamed milk poured first into a glass, followed by a slow espresso shot that settles into distinct visual layers.

### Caffè Mocha

A chocolate-flavored latte made with espresso, steamed milk, and chocolate syrup or cocoa powder, often topped with whipped cream.

## Filter, Drip & Alternative Brewing

### Americano

Espresso diluted with hot water (1:2 to 1:4 ratio), matching the strength of drip coffee while keeping the espresso flavor profile.

### Long Black

Popular in Australasia; similar to an Americano, but hot water is poured first and espresso is extracted on top to preserve the crema layer.

### Pour-Over / Filter Coffee (V60, Chemex)

Hot water slowly hand-poured over coarse grounds in a paper filter, producing a clean, light-bodied cup that highlights single-origin nuances.

### French Press (Cafetière)

Coarsely ground coffee steeped directly in hot water and separated by a mesh plunger, yielding a rich, heavy-bodied brew with natural oils.

## Cultural & Regional Specialties

### Turkish / Arabic Coffee

Unfiltered coffee made by boiling extremely fine grounds in a copper pot (cezve/ibrik), served thick with grounds settled at the bottom.

### Vietnamese Iced Coffee (Cà Phê Sữa Đá)

Dark roast coffee brewed through a metal drip filter (phin) directly over sweet condensed milk and served over ice.

### Café au Lait

A French classic combining equal parts strong dark drip/filter coffee (rather than espresso) and warm milk.

### Affogato

An Italian dessert-coffee hybrid featuring a scoop of vanilla gelato “drowned” in a shot of hot espresso.

## Cold & Iced Drinks

### Cold Brew

Coffee grounds steeped in cold water for 12 to 24 hours, resulting in a smooth, naturally sweet, low-acidity cold coffee.
